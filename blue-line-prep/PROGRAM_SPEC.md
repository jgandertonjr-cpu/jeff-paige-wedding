## 3. Safety model (applies in every phase)

**Daily shin battery, every day, about 8 min.** Single-leg balance 3×30–45 s/leg · knee-to-wall 2×10/leg · resisted toe flexion (FDL) 3×15–20/foot · tib raises 3×15–25 with a 3 s lowering · soft-tissue work on calf, peroneals and arch (not on the tibial border). Short-foot dome 2×8/foot on Mon, Wed and Fri. The battery is dropped on test day and the day before.

**Impact gate (`impactGate`), rated during the session as pain plus "shape" over the first 10 min:**
- 0/10 → run as prescribed.
- 1–2/10 that settles within 10 min AND this morning's first steps were 0/10 → run, add nothing.
- 1–2/10 that plateaus, any 3–4/10, or a non-zero morning → swap the run to the bike. Strength and prehab stay.
- Pain that climbs, or ≥5/10 → stop on the spot and treat it as a flare. ≥6/10 → stop all loading and get seen.

**Morning gate (`morningGate`), first steps out of bed:** 0 = dose tolerated, may progress · 1–2 clearing within 30 min = hold, do not progress · >2, lasting past 30 min, or new tenderness = drop back one step and hold for 2 sessions.

**7-day trend rule.** Any pain day in the last 7 removes the speed reps, jumps and the 1.5 check. 3 or more pain days in 7 triggers the flare protocol.

**Red flags (8):** focal tenderness under 5 cm · anterior cortex pain · night or rest pain · pain that worsens through a run · focal hop pain · pain on walking · escalation despite 7 days unloaded · neuro symptoms or swelling. Calisthenics red flags (5): cola urine (rhabdo), abdominal rigidity, radicular back pain, radicular neck pain, hernia signs.

**Flare tiers.** T1 gate fail → bike at equal duration for about 1 week. T2 4–5/10 or trend rule → no impact for at least 7 days, strength −30%, typically 3–4 weeks. T3 red flag → stop and get imaged. Academy: tell PT staff the same day and zero everything optional. A second flare inside 6 weeks means re-phase.

**Return-to-run ladder (`RTR`), 8 stages on alternate days, advancing only on a 0/10 session plus a 0/10 morning:** walk 25 → 4×1 min jog in a 20 min walk → 6×1 min → 8 min continuous → 10 → 12 + 2 strides → 15 with a 3 min pace block → 18 + 4×60 m. Re-entry at stage 5–6 if the base is intact.

**Substitutions (`subFor`).** Bike at 1.5× the run minutes; a hard run becomes ≥45 min threshold bike. Rower at 1.3× (blocked on sit-up days). Walk at 2.5×, split across the day.

**Sunday screen (every week):** tenderness map in cm with a photo · 10 single-leg hops per leg · calf capacity test in Weeks 1, 4, 7 and 9 (target 25–30+ per leg within 10% by 13 Dec).

**Weekly running cap `WEEK_MIN_CAP` = 70 min (own plus academy).** Build-to-build ramp ≤10% and ≤+5 min against the highest of the last 4 non-deload weeks. A collapse detector flags a week ≥130% of the trailing mean followed by a ≥50% drop.

- **Goal-pace reps** run at "next-step" pace = 3% faster than the last logged 1.5, never faster than the 11:58 goal pace. From 13:12 that is **8:32/mi (≈12:48 1.5)**, with walk recoveries equal to the rep. It recalculates when a new 1.5 is logged.
- **Cadence:** metronome +5–7% on every easy run in Wks 1–4, on Wed and Sat in Wks 5–8, then a Watch check on Saturdays.
- **Surface:** grass or track. The curved TrueForm is limited to ≤ half of a run through Week 4 and is never used for strides. Asphalt steps on the long run follow `ASPHALT` above.
- **300 m:** only 2 full efforts allowed before the academy (`MAX_300_EFFORTS`): the mock PFT plus one spare.
- Peak pre-academy running is **65 min/wk, longest run 25 min.**

### 4b. Barbell wave (`WAVE`, sets × reps @ %e1RM)

| Wk | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4DL | 5 | 6 | 7DL | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | 3×5 @75 | 4×5 @77.5 | 4×4 @82.5 | 2×5 @70 | 4×5 @80 | 4×3 @85 | 2×5 @70 | 4×5 @80 | 4×3 @87.5 | 3×2 @90 | 2×3 @80 | 2×2 @80 |

- Squat (Tue), deadlift (Thu), bench (Mon) and OHP (Fri), each once a week. e1RM is calculated with Epley plus reps-in-reserve from the logged last set. It rises on any higher reading and falls only after 2 consecutive readings ≥5% lower. Deload and taper logs are ignored.
- On build weeks, add 1–2 reps to the last set if it felt like 3+ reps were left. Until an e1RM is logged, the app prescribes "work up to 1×5 @ ~RPE 8".
- Soleus: 4×6–8 at an 8RM (2 sets on deloads, 3 in the academy), +10 lb when every set hits 8.

### 4c. Push-up and sit-up engine

- Doses are calculated from the most recent logged 60 s max (seeded at 26 and 35).
- **Push-ups, 3 sessions a week:** Mon clusters · Wed "test-pace set" (×2: reps = 70% of max at 33/min with a 5 s locked-arm hold every 10) · Fri clusters. Cluster reps = 55% of max (Wk 1–2) → 60% (Wk 3) → 65% (Wk 4–6) → 70% (Wk 7+); deload = 85% of the prior week's. Weekly cap ≤ min(350, 13× max).
- **Sit-ups, 3 sessions a week:** Mon test-pace set ×2 (75% of max at 40/min, 5 s sit-up hold every 10) · Wed sets (≤20 reps a set, about 3 left in reserve) · Fri technique set 2× ~55% of max at 40/min, plus from Wk 5 one fast set of 22 reps in ~30 s.
- AMRAPs only in Wks 1, 4 and 7, at the mock PFT (Wk 11), and on academy deload Saturdays. 15 s splits are recorded.
- Technique coaching built in: "drop, don't lower", "hands are a hat", heels hooked, no sit-ups within 90 min of waking, early and brief locked-arm rests.
- **Example at max 26/35 (Week 10):** Mon 5×18 push-ups + 2×26 sit-ups · Wed 2×18 test-pace push-ups + 4×20 sit-ups · Fri 5×18 push-ups + 2×19 sit-ups + 1×22 fast. Note that **18 = 70% of 26**, so these doses only grow once a higher max is logged.
- Accessories: neck endurance Mon/Wed/Fri, hip-flexor iso Tue/Fri.

### 4d. Bike and rower

- Heart rate is the primary target (Karvonen: resting 52, max 188 estimated). Z2 ride = HR 134–147.
- Tue steady row 25–36 min · Wed Z2 bike 25–36 min · Thu hard bike 45 min, rotating threshold (×6 min @ HR 161–172), VO2 (×3 min → 172–181), climb (×5 min, 60–70 rpm) and tempo. Deload rides are 25 min.
- Total bike + row: about 100 min/wk on build weeks, 75 on deloads.

### 4e. Tissue-tolerance progressions

- **Duty boots (`BOOT_MIN`, walk minutes, 2 walks a week):** Wk 1 break-in indoors → 10 → 15 → 15 → 20 → 25 → 20 → 30 → 35 → 40. Walks on Tue + Fri, moving to Tue + Thu from Wk 8. Hours worn on the Tuesday from Wk 6: 2, 2, 3, 4, 5. Full ~6 h day in boots Sat 26 Dec.
- **Jump prep (`JUMPS`, Tue + Thu before lifting, only on pain-free weeks):** Wk 3 2×10 pogos → Wk 10 2×20 pogos, 4×30 jacks, 2×5 jump burpees. Cap 80 pogo+burpee contacts. None in the academy ("the academy supplies it").
- **Formation-pace finish** on the Sat long run, Wks 8–10: last 6, 8 and 10 min at ~10:00/mi on asphalt.

- **Academy wave:** Wks 13–14 2×3 @75%. Then a rotation of 3×5 @80 / 3×3 @85 / 3×4 @82.5, with the top set capped at RPE 8 (stop with 2 reps left). Deloads 2×5 @70.

- **Academy test taper (`ACAD_TAPER_WINDOW` = 7 days), fires only if you enter a test date:** T−7 to T−5 moderate upper body only (bench 2×3 @80%, chins, soleus 2×8, pacing sets) · T−4/T−3 battery + optional spin · T−2 pattern touch · T−1 walk · T-day warm-up.
- **Nutrition in Phase C:** maintenance in Wks 13–14, then a deficit of ≤250 kcal.
- There is **no planned graduation peak or taper**. The calendar ends 27 Jun, and graduation (~8 Jun) is not encoded.

## 7. Nutrition engine (Fuel tab)

- Protein target fixed at 200–220 g. Fat ≥0.7 g/kg; carbs make up the rest.
- The deficit is capped by an energy-availability floor (30 kcal/kg FFM hard, 33 preferred) and by these overrides: shins symptomatic in the last 7 days → ≤250 kcal · flare → maintenance · sleep under 6.5 h → ≤250 · first 2 academy weeks → maintenance · rest of the academy → ≤250 · taper/test windows → per code.
- Inputs: bodyweight, body-fat %, training kcal/day, activity multiplier (default 1.375) and an optional NEAT factor. Bodyweight is tracked as an exponentially weighted trend.

## 8. Gaps observed during extraction (facts, not yet recommendations)

1. **Landscaping work is not modelled anywhere.** There is no field, multiplier or load term for daytime physical work. The nutrition multiplier defaults to 1.375 (lightly active).
2. **The academy is modelled as 5 PT mornings a week.** `acadPT` is placed Mon–Fri. The brief says ~2/week is the more likely default. There is no setting for PT days per week or which weekdays.
3. **Own running is 0 for all 26 academy weeks** under the default assumption, and 1.5 and 300 m work disappears with it. That is consistent with "academy PT is the training load", and inconsistent with "academy PT alone will not maintain fitness."
4. **Peak pre-academy running is 65 min/wk with a longest run of 25 min.** Whether that is enough to hold 11:58 (goal) or even to bank margin on 13:08 has not been tested beyond the Wk 6 check and the mock.
5. **The 1.5 pass line is 13:16, not MCCC's 13:08.** Both still need re-verification.
6. **The calendar runs past graduation (Wk 35 ≈ 7–11 Jun)**, with no graduation peak or taper unless a date is entered.
7. **Current numbers are unknown to the repo.** Logged retests (Wk 1 AMRAP, Wk 6 check) live only in the phone's localStorage.
