How this works

Change the website RSVP responses

You can change the wedding website by typing what you want, the way you would text Jeff. Nothing on the site changes until you tap Publish.

Signing in

Open the wedding website and add /admin to the end of the address. Type the password Jeff gave you and tap Sign in. Tap Show if you want to check what you typed.

You stay signed in on that phone for 30 days. If you type the password wrong five times, wait 15 minutes and try again.

Tip: on an iPhone, tap the Share button and then Add to Home Screen, so the editor opens like an app.

Asking for a change

Type what you would like in the box at the bottom and tap Send. Plain words are best. Say which page if it isn't obvious.

A few seconds later you'll see Here's what will change, with a short list of every change. Lines marked Please check are worth a second look. Then choose:

  • Publish puts the change on the website. It's live in about a minute.
  • Cancel leaves the website exactly as it is.
  • See it first gets a private preview of the site with your change, so you can look before anyone else does. It takes about a minute.

Some changes affect how a page looks, not just its words. For those you'll see This is a bigger change, and the preview is made for you first. Publish appears once the preview is ready, so open it and have a look.

If I'm not sure what you mean, I'll ask you a question. Just answer in the same box. Here are some things you can ask for:

  • Change the welcome message on the home page to say "We can't wait to celebrate with you."
  • Change the hotel booking deadline to June 1.
  • Change the after party start time to 9 pm.
  • Add a question to the FAQ: Is there parking? Yes, there is free parking at the venue.
  • Swap the top photo on the home page for this one (with a photo added).
  • Make the green a little darker everywhere on the site.
  • On the Travel page, move the airports section above the hotels.
  • Fix the spelling of Aunt Carol's name on the guest list.
  • Open RSVPs, and ask for replies by May 1.
  • I'm not sure how to word the dress code for the after party. Can you suggest something?

Adding a photo

Tap the picture button to the left of the box and choose a photo from your library. A small copy appears above the box so you can see it's the right one; tap Remove if it isn't.

Then say where it should go, for example "Put this photo at the top of the Our Story page." The photo is made the right size for the website for you. Add one photo at a time.

Undoing a change

Right after a change goes live you'll see It's live, with See the site and Undo this. Tap Undo this to put back what was there before.

For anything older, tap Recent changes at the top of the editor. Each change is listed with its date. Tap Undo this on the one you want, then Yes, undo.

Undo only puts back what that change replaced. If part of it has been changed again since, I leave that part alone and tell you which.

If something looks wrong

  • The website stays exactly as it was unless you tapped Publish. Closing the page, cancelling, or a message that something went wrong never changes it.
  • If you published something you don't like, tap Undo this, on the change itself or in Recent changes.
  • If a message says to try again, wait a minute and send it again. There is also a daily limit on requests; it resets the next day.
  • If a change you publish doesn't work on the website, the website keeps showing the last good version, and I take the change back by myself: straight away while this page is open, or the next time anyone uses it.
  • If anything still seems stuck or confusing, tell Jeff.
