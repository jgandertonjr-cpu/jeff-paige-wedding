The wedding site, brought into line with DESIGN.md and refined. Copy, photos, page structure, navigation and functionality are unchanged.

Summary

What changed

Every color, gradient, font, type size, spacing step, radius, shadow and duration is now a token in styles.css, and every hex value in the stylesheet comes from DESIGN.md. Pinyon Script appears only on the “&”. Rosette appears only on RSVP buttons. Nothing animates longer than 300ms. All text pairings were measured at WCAG AA or better.

Deviations & later ideas

Remaining deviations from DESIGN.md

  • Homepage opens on ivory, not a sage banner. Kept on purpose (D5): the arch photo and names are the composition, and the sage countdown band directly below acts as the banner.
  • 404 has no banner or footer. Adding them would change page structure, which is locked.
  • Form error red #A33B2E. Not in the palette; validation needs a distinct color. 6.2:1 on ivory.
  • Desktop film-grain overlay at 5% opacity. Invisible as texture, kept; remove if you read it as decoration.
  • Hero photo is 683px wide shown at 440px, so slightly soft on retina. Photos are locked.

Still open

  • Higher-resolution photos. No larger source exists for the hero photo or the two building photos on Our Story. The engagement photo’s original in design/originals is larger, but the site copy already covers its display size at 2×. Send larger files when you have them.
  • Registry card styles were removed as unused. When a registry goes live, cards should use the standard card style with a link.
