# Changing the wedding website

Find the thing you want to change, change it, and look over your changes. Nothing on the site changes until you tap **Publish**.

## Signing in

Open the wedding website and add **/admin** to the end of the address. Type the password Jeff gave you and tap **Sign in**. You stay signed in on that phone for 30 days. If you type the password wrong five times, wait 15 minutes and try again.

## Finding what to change

Type a few words in the box under **What would you like to change?**, or tap a page under **Or pick a page**. Tap a match to open it. If nothing matches, try different words. Here are ten searches that work:

1. **hotel deadline** finds the room block booking deadline.
2. **ceremony time** finds the ceremony time on the Details page.
3. **parking** finds the parking details and the parking question in the FAQ.
4. **dress code** finds the dress code for the ceremony and for the after party.
5. **registry link** finds the registry link.
6. **wedding date** finds the wedding date.
7. **pink** finds the pink colours.
8. **Avery** (any guest's first name) finds that guest on the guest list.
9. **welcome** finds the welcome message on the home page.
10. **footer** finds the footer text at the bottom of every page.

## Changing it

Each thing has the right control: a box for words, a date picker, a time box, an on/off switch, a colour picker, or a choice list. Make your change and tap **Save change**. It goes into your tray.

Lists, such as the FAQ, schedule, hotels, wedding party and gallery, show each item with **Edit**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove**, and **Add one** at the bottom. When you finish editing an item, tap **Save changes** (**Add it** for a new one).

## Photos

Pick a photo already on the site, or tap **Choose from my phone**. You can add three new photos at a time. Each one is made the right size for you.

## Reviewing and publishing

The bar at the bottom shows how many changes are ready. Tap **Review** to see **Here's what will change**. Lines marked **Please check** are worth a second look. Then choose:

- **Publish** puts it on the website. When you see **It's live**, it's done.
- **Cancel** leaves the website exactly as it is. Your changes stay in the tray.
- **See it first** gets a private preview.

## Undoing a change

Right after publishing, tap **Undo this**. For an older change, tap **Recent changes** at the top, then **Undo this** on the one you want and **Yes, undo**.

## If something looks wrong

- Nothing changes unless you tap **Publish**.
- Published something you don't like? Tap **Undo this**.
- If a change doesn't work on the site, it is put back for you.
- Things like button shapes or the layout of a page can't be changed here. Ask Jeff.
- Still stuck? Tell Jeff.
