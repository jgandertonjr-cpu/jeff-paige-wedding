- **Right:** the safety model is excellent and stays: the daily shin battery, the shape-reading impact gate, the morning progression gate, 3:1 deloads, the bike as the aerobic engine, the asphalt steps, the boot progression, the Week 11 mock in test order, and the 2-token 300 m limit.

- **Wrong (minor):** the deficit runs straight through peak load and the mock, and the academy phase defaults to a ≤250 kcal deficit. Fat loss should stop on 14 Dec, and maintenance should be the academy default.
- **Wrong:** Phase C assumes PT 5 mornings a week and sets own running to **0 min for all 26 academy weeks**. That leaves no 1.5 or 300 work, one pacing set a week, no graduation peak, and a calendar that runs 3 weeks past graduation.
- **Wrong:** Weeks 8–10 stack too much at once. Week 8 adds seven new loads, longest runs break the app's own Frandsen +10% rule, 2×200 and 250+150 sprints come on top, and the strength wave peaks at 90% for a test that has no lift.
- **Wrong:** push-up and sit-up doses freeze at 70% of a max that has never been tested. The cadence is set exactly at the goal (33/min, 40/min), so there is no margin.
- **Blunt:** his 13:12 in September **fails** MCCC's 13:08 line by 4 s. The 1.5 is the event most likely to remove him.
- **Risk 1: an MTSS flare** from stacked load: Week 8's stacking, uncontrolled academy running spikes on a 245 lb frame, and long academy days on his feet that his current routine doesn't prepare him for.
- **Risk 2: detraining the 1.5 inside the academy.** Falling below the 30th percentile at a "periodic" assessment means removal.
- **Risk 3: the low back and the obstacle course.** About 190 anchored sit-ups a week plus a heavy deadlift and carries, with no preparation for the 5 ft wall at 245 lb.

**Test rules:** order is sit-ups → 300 m → push-ups → 1.5 mi, ≥5 min between events, all within 2 h. Failing any one event fails the test [B][C].

**Do exit standards differ from entry?**
- **Fitness numbers: no.** Current material is titled "Entrance and Retention", and cadets "must maintain the 30th percentile…throughout the academy until completion" [B][A]. The 2014 50th-percentile graduation standard (40/56.0/33/11:58) is not in current material [D]. When it was dropped is not documented.
- **Obstacle course: yes, it is an added requirement.** "To graduate, you'll have to pass an obstacle course," with three chances [C]. The MPO-245 elements, in order [F]:
  1. Exit a patrol car from the driver's seat with the belt buckled.
  2. 6-inch low obstacles with direction changes.
  3. A 5 ft solid wall with no handles.
  4. A 36 in × 3 ft obstacle.
  5. Drag a 165 lb dummy behind the wall.
  6. Carry a 25 lb medicine ball to the finish.
- Each element is scored satisfactory or unsatisfactory, and the time is recorded. No time limit is published.

**Mid-academy retests:**
- **Yes.** pa.gov describes "periodic fitness assessments". Falling below standard means "removed from the academy until you can meet the standard again" [C].
- **Frequency is unpublished** by MPOETC or MCCC.
- The instructor reads the MPOETC rules' "first and second" and "final" assessments as **3 assessments**, each followed by an obstacle-course attempt. HACC also assesses 3 times [G].
- Provisional windows, inferred from the Westmoreland syllabus and **not published**: Wks 13–14 (4–17 Jan), Wks 23–24 (15–28 Mar), Wks 33–34 (24 May–6 Jun).

**13:08 vs 13:16, resolved:**
- 13:16 is the current statewide standard: the 2024 chart and the live pa.gov page [B][C].
- 13:15 is the 2014 chart [D].
- 13:08 appears only on MCCC's page [A]. No MPOETC source for it exists, and only the academy can say whether it is a typo or a house rule.
- **Train and judge against 13:08.** The app's pass line should show 13:08, with 13:16 noted.
- Act 37 of 2023 (the 15th-percentile bar) applies only to Philadelphia [H]. 37 Pa. Code §203.11 is outdated [K].

**Turning 30:**
- Standards apply by "age at the time of testing" [B][C], so after his 30th birthday the 30–39 numbers (32/63/20/13:46) would technically apply.
- This is not confirmed for in-academy retests. **Plan to the 18–29 standards regardless.**

**Unconfirmed:** the source of 13:08; MCCC's PT days, hours and content; formation-run length and pace; assessment and obstacle-course dates; retest frequency and failure rules; PT footwear and boots; which age bracket applies after turning 30; the exact graduation date. The application PDF gives 7 Jun; the June 2026 class graduated 10 Jun [J].

## 14. App implementation notes (`src/blue-line-prep.html`; no redesign)

### Data safety

- History lives in `localStorage` key **`blp-v6`**: done, pain, screen, subs, rtr, flags, scores, weights, efforts, runLog, body, tests, lifts, acad, acadAssume.
- **Check-offs are positional** (`trackKeys` → `${week}-${dow}-am-${i}`). Reordering or adding items in a day shifts its keys.
- Therefore:
  - **Gate every structural change behind `week >= 5`** (a `NEW_FROM = 5` constant). Weeks 1–4 keep building exactly as today, so completed check-offs are not orphaned.
  - Week 4 tweaks are text-only (the bike-sprint 50 s line, the boot-walk Off-day note), with no added or removed items.
  - New state (`ptDays`, `ptTemplate`, `readiness`, `ftp`) is added to the saved object with defaults on load (`d.ptDays ?? [1,3]`). Keep `v:6`. No migration.
  - Do not recompute history: the `e1Track` +5% cap and the trend redefinition apply to entries after the change date. The trend change can only clear flags, never add them.
  - `DELOADS` and ceiling changes touch only weeks ≥13.
