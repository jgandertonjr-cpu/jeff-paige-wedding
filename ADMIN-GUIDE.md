# Changing the wedding website

You can change the website by typing what you want, the way you would text Jeff. Nothing on the site changes until you tap **Publish**.

## Signing in

Open the wedding website and add **/admin** to the end of the address. Type the password Jeff gave you and tap **Sign in**. You stay signed in on that phone for 30 days. If you type the password wrong five times, wait 15 minutes and try again.

## Asking for a change

Type what you would like in the box at the bottom and tap **Send**. Plain words are best. Say which page if it isn't obvious. If I'm not sure what you mean, I'll ask. Just answer in the same box.

A few seconds later you'll see **Here's what will change**, with a short list. Lines marked **Please check** are worth a second look. Then choose:

- **Publish** puts the change on the website. It's live in about a minute.
- **Cancel** leaves the website exactly as it is.
- **See it first** opens a private preview so you can look before anyone else does.

Changes to how a page looks, not just its words, always get a preview first. **Publish** appears once it's ready.

## Ten example requests

1. Change the welcome message on the home page to say "We can't wait to celebrate with you."
2. Change the hotel booking deadline to June 1.
3. Change the after party start time to 9 pm.
4. Add a question to the FAQ: Is there parking? Yes, there is free parking at the venue.
5. Swap the top photo on the home page for this one (with a photo added).
6. Make the green a little darker everywhere on the site.
7. On the Travel page, move the airports section above the hotels.
8. Fix the spelling of Aunt Carol's name on the guest list.
9. Open RSVPs, and ask for replies by May 1.
10. I'm not sure how to word the dress code for the after party. Can you suggest something?

## Adding a photo

Tap the picture button beside the box and choose a photo. A small copy appears so you can check it's the right one; tap **Remove** if it isn't. Then say where it should go, for example "Put this photo at the top of the Our Story page." The photo is made the right size for you. Add one at a time.

## Undoing a change

Right after a change goes live you'll see **It's live**, with **Undo this**. For anything older, tap **Recent changes** at the top, then **Undo this** on the one you want and **Yes, undo**. If part of it has been changed again since, that part is left alone and I tell you which.

## If something looks wrong

- The website stays as it was unless you tapped **Publish**. Closing the page, cancelling, or an error message never changes it.
- Published something you don't like? Tap **Undo this**.
- If a message says to try again, wait a minute and send it again. There is a daily limit on requests; it resets the next day.
- If a change you publish doesn't work on the website, the website keeps showing the last good version, and I take the change back by myself: straight away while this page is open, or the next time anyone uses it.
- Still stuck or confused? Tell Jeff.
